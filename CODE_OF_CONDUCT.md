# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Archflow to be a project people are glad to contribute to, whatever their background or
experience level. Everyone taking part in this project — in issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other space it uses — agrees to make that possible.

## What we expect

- **Assume competence and good faith.** People arrive with different context, not less intelligence.
- **Critique the work, not the person.** "This breaks the mirror rule" is useful. "You clearly did
  not read the docs" is not.
- **Be specific.** A review that says what is wrong and why helps; one that says it is bad does not.
- **Accept that maintainers say no.** Scope is a real constraint, and a declined change is not a
  judgement of the person who proposed it.
- **Say when you are unsure.** Uncertainty stated plainly is worth more than confidence that has to
  be walked back.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Insults, demeaning comments, or attacks based on personal characteristics, including but not
  limited to age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance,
  race, religion, or sexual orientation.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, or misrepresenting what a change does.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project uses, and whenever someone is representing the project in
public.

## Reporting

Report anything that concerns you to the maintainer at the address in
[`.claude-plugin/marketplace.json`](.claude-plugin/marketplace.json), or by opening a private
security advisory if a public issue would make things worse.

Reports are handled confidentially. You will get an acknowledgement, and we will tell you what we
decided. You do not have to be the target of the behaviour to report it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that do not fit these guidelines, and will say why.
Repeated or serious violations lead to a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.

Enforcement should be proportionate. Most problems are a misunderstanding and end with a
conversation. The measures above exist for the cases that do not.
